Innovation Activity in Clean Energy Sectors for 2024.

Source: World Economic Forum (WEF), Wikipedia - Renewable Energy

The pie chart titled "Innovation Activity by Sector" visualizes the distribution of research and development activity across key clean energy sectors for 2024. According to the data:

  • Solar energy leads with the highest innovation activity score of 95, reflecting significant investment in advanced photovoltaics, perovskite materials, and solar panel efficiency.
  • Battery technologies follow closely with a score of 90, emphasizing ongoing efforts in solid-state batteries and large-scale grid storage solutions.
  • Wind energy has an activity score of 85, boosted by next-gen turbine designs and offshore installations.
  • Hydrogen, scoring 75, is gaining momentum as a clean fuel, especially in sectors hard to decarbonize like shipping and heavy industry.
  • Hydropower, with a score of 50, reflects steady but comparatively lower innovation, as much of the infrastructure is mature and well-established.

These scores represent not just financial investments, but also the intensity of R&D, policy focus, and technological advancement within each sector. The data illustrates how the clean energy landscape is evolving with a strong tilt toward solar and storage technologies in 2024.
